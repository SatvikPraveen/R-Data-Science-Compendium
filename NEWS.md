# RDataScienceCompendium 0.2.0

First release as an installable, tested R package and research compendium.

## New features

* `boot_ci()`: percentile, basic, normal and BCa bootstrap intervals,
  numerically identical to `boot::boot.ci()`; `jackknife_influence()`.
* `perm_test()`: exact or Monte Carlo two-sample permutation and
  one-sample/paired sign-flip tests with Phipson-Smyth p-values.
* `cohens_d()`, `hedges_g()`, `hedges_correction()`: standardised mean
  differences with exact noncentral-t intervals.
* `run_simulation()`, `sim_performance()`, `sim_n_required()`,
  `rng_streams()`: reproducible simulation studies with per-replicate
  L'Ecuyer-CMRG streams, captured failures, and Morris et al. (2019)
  performance measures with Monte Carlo SEs.
* `make_folds()`, `cross_validate()`, `nested_cv()`: stratified, grouped and
  nested cross-validation, reporting fold-averaged and pooled estimates.
* `auc()`, `auc_ci()` (DeLong), `brier_score()`, `log_loss()`,
  `calibration()`, `rmse()`, `mae()`, `r_squared()`.
* `simulate_linear()`, `simulate_logistic()`, `ar1_cor()`: data generators
  with known ground truth.

## Research compendium

* `analysis/`: two ADEMP simulation studies (interval coverage under skew;
  optimism of cross-validation after model selection) with committed
  results, figures and a paper, regenerated by `make analysis`.
* Three vignettes covering resampling inference, simulation studies and
  model evaluation.

## Infrastructure

* R CMD check on Linux, macOS and Windows (R 4.1 to devel), coverage,
  lintr and pkgdown workflows.
* Reproducible Docker image and a real `renv.lock`.
* The previous tutorial scripts moved from `R/` to `modules/`.
